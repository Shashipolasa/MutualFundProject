\# Data Dictionary



\## Fund Master



amfi\_code - unique fund code



fund\_house - company managing the fund



scheme\_name - name of mutual fund scheme



category - equity or debt



sub\_category - large cap, mid cap, small cap etc.



expense\_ratio\_pct - expense ratio charged by fund



risk\_category - risk level of fund



\## NAV History



amfi\_code - fund code



date - NAV date



nav - net asset value



\## Investor Transactions



investor\_id - investor identifier



transaction\_date - date of transaction



transaction\_type - SIP, Lumpsum or Redemption



amount\_inr - transaction amount



state - investor state



city - investor city



payment\_mode - payment method



kyc\_status - KYC verification status



\## Scheme Performance



return\_1yr\_pct - 1 year return



return\_3yr\_pct - 3 year return



return\_5yr\_pct - 5 year return



aum\_crore - assets under management



expense\_ratio\_pct - expense ratio



risk\_grade - risk rating
